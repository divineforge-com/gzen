gZen makes small, free Go programs for a calm mind: terminal tools, little games that end with a saying,
and essays on simplicity, patience and clean code. Zen is the voice. Go is the craft.

Everything here is built in the open by [DivineForge](https://divineforge.com/). The tools are MIT licensed
and live on [GitHub](https://github.com/divineforge).
